Featured · Clinical AI · 2025

Clinical intelligence & workflow optimiser

A neurologist should not spend 35 minutes writing notes after a 20-minute consultation. I built the voice-to-SOAP pipeline that brings it to 18.

  • Containerised on AWS with Supabase, under HIPAA-compliant PII governance: redaction before storage, three client demos delivered.

Forecasting · 2024 — ongoing

S&P 500 price forecasting

23,323 daily records from 1927 to 2024, and an honest look at how far a sequence model actually gets.

  • Cleaning, redundancy analysis, IQR-based outlier capping and min–max scaling on adjacent close and volume.
  • LSTM with a 60-day look-back window and a time-based 80/20 split — evaluated on RMSE, MAPE and directional accuracy, not just loss.

Network analysis · 2024

Protein interactions in NSCLC

4.9M STRING interactions reduced to 184K high-confidence edges — then asked which proteins matter.

  • Degree, closeness, betweenness and eigenvector centrality with Louvain community detection, surfacing eight functional modules.
  • TP53 was the strongest hub across every measure; KRAS and AKT1 mapped onto MAPK and PI3K/AKT pathways, feeding proposed multi-target combinations.
  • NetworkX
  • igraph
  • pandas
  • seaborn

LLM tooling · 2024

AI email generator

Writing the same email forty times a week, solved once.

  • Streamlit front end with an OpenAI backend: two drafts and five subject lines per request, configurable tone and length, SQLite for history and favourites.
  • 60+ unit tests, structured logging, exponential-backoff retries for rate limits, and per-generation cost estimates. Users reported ~70% faster composition.
  • Streamlit
  • OpenAI API
  • SQLite
  • pytest

Computer vision · 2024

Driver drowsiness detection

Fatigue shows up in the face before it shows up in the driving.

  • dlib 68-point landmarks with engineered Eye-Aspect-Ratio and Mouth-Open-Ratio features, thresholded over a rolling window so a blink isn’t an alert.
  • ~93% accuracy at a 7% false-positive rate, 30–50 ms per frame on ordinary CPU hardware.
  • OpenCV
  • dlib
  • NumPy

Also built: YouTube trending analytics across four countries (~40K records — the 4–7 minute sweet spot is real) and a Spotify engagement regression that lifted R² from 0.04 to 0.42 with genre and tempo as the drivers. More on GitHub ↗

04 — About

Why the pipelines, not just the models

The models were never the hard part. The hard part is the third time someone asks where a number came from and the answer has to be true, reproducible, and fast enough to sit in a meeting. That’s the work I keep choosing: ELT that people trust, dashboards that answer a question without a Slack thread, and AI features that degrade gracefully instead of beautifully.

I came to it through computer engineering in Bangalore and a master’s in data science in Chicago, and I’ve since worked across supply chain, healthcare, banking-fraud and cybersecurity data. Different industries, same problem shape — messy operational truth that somebody needs to act on by Thursday.

Outside work I’m a trained Carnatic vocalist (about ten years, six-plus live performances), I hosted a podcast called What is Data Science? aimed at people who never asked, and I’m happiest explaining a graph to someone who is about to ask “but what does it mean?”
